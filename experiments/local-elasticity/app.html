What this experiment establishes

A local implementation of FreeForm’s reduced elasticity, coupled to Three r186 splat rendering. Material values and volume are assumptions; a scan does not reveal real stiffness or hidden interiors. No fracture or self-collision. This is a research prototype, not a new physics algorithm.
