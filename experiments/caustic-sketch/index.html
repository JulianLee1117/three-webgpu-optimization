A DRAWING BECOMES GEOMETRY

One piece of glass.
Thousands of tiny lenses.

The drawing tells an optimizer where light should land. It changes the surface of a glass slab until refracted rays gather into that shape.

Three.js WebGPU then traces 16,384 light samples through that surface. The screen receives only those samples; it never receives your drawing as a texture.

Inverse caustic design is established research. This experiment combines it with a live browser drawing tool, a Three.js TSL light renderer, and a separate ray tracer for checking the result.

The model uses an ideal parallel beam, a fixed refractive index of 1.49, and one transmitted path through a flat entry face and a curved exit face. It omits diffraction and internal reflections. The exported mesh approximates the optimized surface; it is an experiment, not a calibrated fabrication design.
