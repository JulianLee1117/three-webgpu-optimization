The correctness canaries use external npm dependencies. The separately generated portable ORT surface demo redistributes the pinned nightly bundle and WASM with their MIT license and upstream third-party notices in its `licenses/` directory.

The ORT surface model is a new 891-byte synthetic wave model generated by this
repository's NumPy training script; it is distinct from the historical ORT test
model. The surface demo's patched Three.js bundles retain Three's MIT notices.
No Three Blocks implementation is included in the portable surface demo.
